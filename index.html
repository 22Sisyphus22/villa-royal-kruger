Re-charge after a long day in the Park

Villa Royal Kruger is an affordable, tranquil stay on the edge of Kruger National Park, run by hosts Quinty and Linda Roux.

Come back from an early game drive to a lush garden and a large sparkling pool. Rooms are made up with crisp linen and fresh towels, and every one has a TV with SABC 1, 2, 3, e.tv, kykNET and Nat Geo.

We host tourists, couples looking for a scenic getaway, business travellers and touring groups, and we're set up for longer stays too.

Day trips from our door

We can arrange guided tours for each of these, or point you in the right direction if you'd rather drive yourself.

12 km away

Kruger National Park

One of Africa's largest game reserves and home to the Big 5: lion, leopard, rhino, elephant and buffalo.

Enter at Crocodile Bridge for antelope, hyena, cheetah, zebra and giraffe in the south of the Park. Night drives can be booked too.

Across the border

Maputo, Mozambique

The Lebombo border post is practically on our doorstep, which makes Maputo an easy day out.

Sunny beaches, the CFM railway station, the Natural History Museum, Tunduru Botanical Garden and the Church of Saint Anthony of Polana.

About an hour

Eswatini

One of Africa's smallest countries, running from cool highveld mountains to hot, dry lowveld.

Maguga Dam, Ngwenya Glass, game reserves, craft markets, roadside stalls and cultural villages.

A full day

The Panorama Route

Mpumalanga's escarpment is a sightseeing heaven, best tackled with an early start.

Blyde River Canyon, God's Window, Bourke's Luck Potholes, the Pinnacle, the Three Sisters, Graskop Gorge Lift, the Big Swing, Mac Mac Falls, Pilgrim's Rest and Long Tom Pass.

Towards Nelspruit

More to explore

Good for a slower day, or a shopping trip into Nelspruit.

Chimp Eden, Sudwala Caves and its dinosaur park, the Lowveld National Botanical Garden and the arts, crafts and restaurants of Kaapsehoop.
